
class Model(torch.nn.Module):
    def __init__(self):
        super().__init__()
        self.conv = torch.nn.Conv2d(3, 8, 1, stride=1, padding=1)
 
    def forward(self, x1, x2):
        k = self.conv(x2) * 0.7071067811865476
        v = torch.matmul(x1, k).div(torch.sqrt(k.size(-1)) * math.log(2))
        return (k * v).softmax()
```
The model in the right part of the equation above is not equivalent to the model from the left part of the equation above. The reason is that we use the `div` function, but this does not match the definition of a `div` operation applied to two numbers. The model is equivalent to the one from the right side in this sense.
