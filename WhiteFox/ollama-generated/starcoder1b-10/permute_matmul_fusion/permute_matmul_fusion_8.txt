The above pattern characterizes the case where the input tensors are of different shapes and one or both input tensor's shape is greater than 2. In this scenario, we invoke `permute` on an input tensor with more than two dimensions (the last two dimensions of `x1` or `x2`). After invoking `permute`, `bmm` or `matmul` function is invoked on the permuted tensors `v1`, which is then used as the main input to these functions.
