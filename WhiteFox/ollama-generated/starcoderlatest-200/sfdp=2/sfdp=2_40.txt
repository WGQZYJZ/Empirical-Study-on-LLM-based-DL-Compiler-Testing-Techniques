This pattern characterizes scenarios where an input tensor t is constructed by concatenation of the query and the key tensor. The bias vector is added to the t tensor in order to simulate a bias term appearing between the attention output of the hyperbolic tangent and the scaled dot product. After calculating the hyperbolic tangent of the tensor t, the attention output of the hyperbolic tangent is multiplied by the value tensor, then softmax is applied on the result of multiplication, finally dropout is applied to the softmax output. After that, the context vector is constructed by a linear transformation (or matrix multiplication) between the concatenated query and key tensor, where the weights are equal to `context_bias * attention_output`.
